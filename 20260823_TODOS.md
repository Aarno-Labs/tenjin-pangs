## 0. Where things stand

The call-graph false-negative class is closed, pending the tail of the sweep. One PIR
lowering fix (constant expressions lowered to real statements, `lowered_constant_exprs`)

precision problem is concentrated in two program shapes:

### T2. Put the FN gate in CI
`metrics/icall_census/fn_gate.sh <census-dir> [bitcode-dir]` already implements criteria
1–4 of the audit doc §8 and exits non-zero on failure. It is not wired to anything.
**Do:** run it per-commit on a small fast subset (the `-O0` modules under ~1 MB complete in

**Why now:** the invariant is currently satisfied. A gate added while green stays cheap;

### T3. Fix the `check-traces` index basis
**Verified defect** (`20260823_ICALL_AUDIT.md` §4.1, repro
`metrics/icall_census/check_traces_index_shift_repro.c`): the runtime numbers indirect calls
per caller over *all* of them, while `check_traces` builds its per-caller site list from
**callgraph rows**. A site with no rows is absent, shifting every later index; the checker
then blames the wrong callsite.
**Do:** build the per-caller list from the callsite table (all indirect sites, `synthetic`
included) and report a site with no rows as its own violation kind.
**Why it still matters** now that silent sites are gone: the shift re-arms the instant a new
FN class appears — exactly when the dynamic checker is the tool being relied on. Fix it
while it is not urgent.
**Files:** `crates/pangs-clients/src/lib.rs` (`check_traces`, `key_ordinal`).

## 2. P1 — the two dominant precision classes

### T4. Dispatch-table icalls that resolve to nothing — **RESOLVED 2026-08-24**

**The framing below was wrong and is corrected first.** "58% of `-O0` sites, 92% of them Ω"
conflates two populations that need different work:

| `-O0` dispatch_table (n=1183) | sites | share |
|---|---:|---:|
| finite | 98 | 8.3% |
| Ω *with* concrete targets | 1042 | 88.1% |
| **Ω-empty ("resolves to nothing")** | **43** | **3.6%** |

The 1,042 are not sites that resolved to nothing: Andersen enumerated targets and the Ω bit
rode along from Steensgaard's boundary verdict, exactly as `DESIGN_lite.md` §2 D′ records.
They belong to T7/T9. The class this item actually describes is the 43. The stated
"Done when" was therefore unreachable as written: clearing every Ω-empty site moves the
dispatch-table Ω share only from 91.7% to 88.1%.

**Root cause — three separable defects, none of them the suspected ones.** The
constant-indexed fn-ptr-array carve-out, B1's `global_place_is_simple`, and the
`Exact`/`Lane`/`Unknown` field domain are all fine; the same offsets resolve exactly when
reached by ordinary stores. `tree`'s `struct listingcalls lc` is populated only by
`lc = (struct listingcalls){ ... }`, which LLVM lowers to a bulk copy from a stack literal:

1. a `Memcpy` edge's prepartition endpoints are both synthetic storage regions, so neither
   address carrier was unioned into the component — the carriers' producers were dropped
   from the admitted solve and the copy ran with an empty source set;
2. the whole-object summary bridge fired only when a dynamic GEP had already materialized
   the summary, so a bulk-copy endpoint exchanged nothing with its field cells;
3. the same bridge gap applied independently to direct whole-object loads and stores.

(1) and (2) are each inert without the other; (3) is independent of both. All three are
fixed and on by default.

**Result.** `exe-tree-O0` and `exe-OMP__tree-O0` each gain 323 concrete call edges as 17
`omega_fnptr` markers resolve (indirect-call unknowns 19 → 2); `pangs differential` goes
from exit 3 to exit 0 there and on `lib-sqlite-O0`. 34 of the 43 Ω-empty `-O0`
dispatch_table sites were this shape. The other 9 are not defects: 5 are genuinely NULL
initializer slots in libusb guarded by `assert()`, parson and ksba are exported-setter hooks
(justified Ω, the T7 category), curl's operand roots at a local, and sqlite's is a
dynamic-index GEP into `@sqlite3BuiltinExtensions` — a separate one-site question about
`Unknown`↔`Exact` aliasing, still open.

**The defect was not confined to icalls.** ModRef has no `unknown_callee |= targets.is_empty()`
net, so the same gap silently under-approximated the write set of any function reached
through an aggregate copy — recovered 165 ModRef rows on `exe-jq-O0` alone, all additive.
Reproducers: `metrics/icall_census/memcpy_aggregate_fnptr_repro.c` and
`memcpy_aggregate_modref_repro.c`.

**Still open from this item:** offset-correspondence projection (the precision half of
`20260730_MEMCPY_HANDLING.md`) — the fix is field-*insensitive*, so `tree` resolves to both
table members rather than the one its offset selects.

### T5. Graduate receiver-payload summaries for receiver-deref dispatch
**Second-largest class: 18% of `-O0` sites, 100% Ω, 0% finite.**

`obj->handler(...)` has neither a dispatch table nor a parameter-passed operand, so neither
of `DESIGN_lite.md` §4's original arguments covered it — and nothing in the default pipeline
resolves any of it. The receiver-allocation-relative payload summaries in §2 D′ are aimed
squarely at this shape and are already implemented behind
`PANGS_ANDERSEN_RECEIVER_PAYLOADS`.

**Do:** measure that flag against the `param_deref` bucket specifically, rather than against
whole-module aggregates. `EXPERIMENT_HISTORY.md` records chibicc's recovered macro-handler
call and a runtime cost that kept the flag opt-in; the census now supplies a population
(362 `-O0` sites, ~2,000 including `-O1`) to evaluate against. If the flag converts a
meaningful share of that bucket, the runtime question becomes a budget question rather than
a value question.

**Hypothesis, not verified:** that receiver payloads will fire on a large share of these
sites. The abstraction requires a certified receiver allocation root within the 16-context
limit; how many real `obj->handler` sites satisfy that is unmeasured. Measure before
committing to graduation.

**Done when:** the fraction of `param_deref` sites the flag converts to finite is known, and
`DESIGN_lite.md` §5's receiver-deref gate can be resolved either way on evidence.

## 3. P2 — make the open questions decidable

### T6. Split `Tier::Simple` into B1 and B2 provenance — **RESOLVED 2026-08-24**
**Verified:** the exact pre-analyses settled **6 of 19,719** indirect callsites corpus-wide,
all six in `exe-vim-9.2-O1`; the then-current confinement census found no confined
functions. `DESIGN_lite.md` §2 credited B2 with ~⅓ of icalls. A current-source rerun on
2026-08-25 supersedes the confinement result: 277 of 14,332 address-taken function
occurrences (1.93%) were confined, in 14 of 56 modules.

The number is now recorded in the docs, but it cannot be *acted* on: B1's InitVal edges and
B2's simple-chain edges both go through `emit_simple_call_edges` and are both tagged
`Tier::Simple` (`crates/pangs-api/src/lib.rs`, `apply_initval_exact_call_edges`), so the six
are unattributed.

**Resolution:** production edges now use `Tier::B1Initval` (`b1_initval`) or
`Tier::B2Simple` (`b2_simple`). Metrics expose `icalls_b1_initval` and
`icalls_b2_simple`; the combined `icalls_simple` counter and legacy `simple` schema value are
retained for additive artifact compatibility. B2 retains precedence at sites both analyses
resolve. The corpus must be rerun to attribute the six previously recorded sites.

**Export evolution:** the callgraph schema accepts `b1_initval` and `b2_simple` in addition
to the legacy `simple` value, and new metrics fields are optional in the schema so older
artifacts remain valid.

### T7. Give Ω a denominator that excludes justified Ω
**Verified confound:** `lib-parson` is 60/60 Ω and *correctly* so — it holds
`static JSON_Malloc_Function parson_malloc = malloc;` behind an exported
`json_set_allocation_functions()`, so in library mode the hook can hold any caller-supplied
function. Ω rate also tracks build mode and module mix far more strongly than any uniform
property of the analysis.

Any target phrased as "reduce Ω" is therefore unmeasurable as stated, and rewards making a
sound answer look better.

**Do:** classify each Ω site as *justified* (an exported setter or an escaping address can
genuinely install an arbitrary target; library-mode entry semantics) or *avoidable* (the
operand's producers are all internal and the analysis simply failed to enumerate them), and
report the split. This is a census extension, not solver work: the existing
`escape_sources` / `unknown_callers` facts should carry most of it.

**Done when:** the census emits an `omega_justified` classification and the coverage
measurements in `DISPOSITION.md` §10 can be stated against the avoidable share.

## 4. P3 — investigations, payoff unknown

### T8. Root-cause B2's near-zero yield
Unblocked by T6; the B1/B2-attributed callsite remeasurement remains to be run. The
2026-08-25 confinement census establishes that B3 is not dead: it found 277 confined
function occurrences, although its 1.93% yield remains far below KELP's 23.9%. It does not
resolve whether B2 is correctly conservative on this corpus or still leaves cheap exact
callsite resolutions on the table. B2 *does* work on the canonical shapes — verified on a
synthetic global-slot probe — so a blanket defect is already ruled out.

### T9. Why closed-producer certificates recover so little
**Verified negative result** (§5 below). Before this is either graduated or dropped,
determine which of these is responsible: the prototype's bounded promotion cap
(8,192 nodes/edges), the rule making aggregate-copy destinations incomplete, or the genuine
openness of the producer graphs on this corpus. The third answer would mean the retained
unknown bits are mostly *correct*, which folds back into T7.

### T10. An ablation harness
The Clash artifact carries a named configuration lattice (`Config`: `Off`, `IntraOnly`,
`DCallOnly`, `ICallType`/`ICallPointer`/`ICallCombined`, each detector alone, each detector
plus inheritance, the full framework) so every mechanism's contribution is separately
measurable. pangs has env flags and `EXPERIMENT_HISTORY.md` but no systematic harness;
`m2-ablation` covers only the B1/B2/B3 grid.

Given how many hypotheses in this session died on contact with data, a general
`pangs ablate --config <name>` over the experimental knobs — receiver payloads, closed
producers, closed consumers, memcpy summaries — looks like good value. Medium effort,
compounding return.

## 5. Tested and rejected — do not re-propose without new evidence

**Closed-producer certificates as the high-leverage switch.** Reasoning from the
near-exact `unknown_callee`/`fallback` agreement, graduating `PANGS_ANDERSEN_CLOSED_PRODUCERS` looked
like the obvious lever. Measured: parson 60 Ω → 60, `exe-tree-O0` 19 → 17, `exe-tmux-O0`
42 → 41, `lib-usb-O0` and `lib-fribidi-O0` unchanged. The hypothesis was wrong. See T9
before revisiting.

**Clash-style per-callsite correlation (the CBC detector).** Clash's callback-constraint
correlates a call's function-pointer operand with its other arguments by callsite, killing
the `dispatch(data, fn)` cross product. It was the most attractive import from that artifact
and pangs has a sound way to build it (per-callsite correlation over KELP-simple chains,
which never pass through memory and so avoid the unsoundness in Clash's tag domain). But it
targets the `param_passed` bucket: **5.8% of `-O0` sites, already 55% resolved.** Lowest
leverage of any option on the table. Revisit only if T4 and T5 land and the residue shifts.

**Tier E.** `DESIGN_lite.md` §5's gate for it is "load-bearing FP edges trace to
parameter-passed fn ptrs that are not Ω-frozen". The census does not support that gate;
see the CBC entry for why.

**Typed heap clones.** The §5 gate is heap-object conflation. Nothing in the census points
there: the classes that fail are global-table loads and receiver derefs, neither of which is
a multi-type-allocation-site problem.

**Also do not re-derive:** Clash's own detectors are unsound as implemented — they prune
real flows when a tag set is incomplete or when a tagged object outlives its invocation, and
`hasClash` is HashSet-iteration-order dependent in both overloads. The transferable content
was the *framing* (context sensitivity as edge filtering rather than domain cloning), not
the mechanism.
